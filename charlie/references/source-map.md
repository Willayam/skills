# Source Map

This map is for navigation and citation labels. The skill references are derived
notes, not a full-text conversion.

- `[Worldly Wisdom 1994]`
- `[Misjudgment 2005]`
- `[Berkshire 2023 Letter]`
- `[Berkshire 2023 Release]`
- `[Berkshire 1996 Letter]`

## Core Sources

### Worldly Wisdom 1994

Charlie Munger, `A Lesson on Elementary Worldly Wisdom As It Relates To
Investment Management & Business`, USC Business School, 1994.

Stable transcript:
`https://fs.blog/great-talks/a-lesson-on-worldly-wisdom/`

Use for:

- Latticework of mental models
- Multiple disciplines
- Avoiding isolated facts
- Hammer/nail model risk
- Circle of competence
- Business quality and investment filtering

### Misjudgment 2005

Charlie Munger, `The Psychology of Human Misjudgment`, revised version prepared
for `Poor Charlie's Almanack`, 2005.

Stable transcript:
`https://fs.blog/great-talks/psychology-human-misjudgment/`

Use for:

- Twenty-five standard causes of human misjudgment
- Incentive-caused bias
- Denial, envy, social proof, commitment and consistency
- Liking, authority, contrast, vivid evidence, stress influence
- Lollapalooza effects

### Berkshire 2023 Letter

Warren Buffett, Berkshire Hathaway 2023 annual report/shareholder letter.

Official PDF:
`https://berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2023ltr.pdf`

Use for:

- Buffett's framing of Munger as the architect of Berkshire
- Quality businesses with enduring economics
- Able and trustworthy managers
- Sitting tight with rare compounding businesses
- Berkshire's conservative balance sheet and anti-fragility

### Berkshire 2023 Release

Berkshire Hathaway news release on Charlie Munger's death, November 28, 2023.

Official PDF:
`https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/nov2823.pdf`

Use for:

- Verified date and official death announcement
- Buffett's statement that Berkshire could not have been built without Munger's
  inspiration, wisdom, and participation

### Berkshire 1996 Letter

Official HTML:
`https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1996.html`

### Berkshire 2012 Report

`https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/2012ar/2012ar.pdf`
