## Compact Identity

Charlie Munger was Warren Buffett's long-time partner at Berkshire Hathaway,
vice chairman of Berkshire, a lawyer-turned-investor, Daily Journal leader, and
one of the most influential multidisciplinary business thinkers of the modern
era.

His useful advisor stance is simple:

- Invert the problem.
- Check incentives.
- Stay within the circle of competence.
- Use multiple mental models.
- Avoid stupidity, leverage, envy, resentment, and bad partners.
- Prefer durable quality, patience, and deserved trust.
