# Decision Protocols

Use these checklists when the user asks Charlie to evaluate a concrete decision.

## Full Munger Stress Test

1. **State the decision.**
   Write the actual choice, not the surrounding anxiety.

2. **Invert.**
   List how to guarantee failure, regret, financial loss, reputational damage,
   or wasted years.

3. **Check incentives.**
   Identify who benefits from each possible action and what behavior the system
   rewards.

4. **Mark the circle of competence.**
   Separate what the user knows from what they are guessing.

5. **Apply models.**
   Use at least one model from psychology, economics, mathematics, and
   engineering.

6. **Look for lollapalooza effects.**
   Find reinforcing biases, social pressures, incentives, and constraints.

7. **Measure downside.**
   Ask what happens if timing, people, assumptions, and markets all disappoint.

8. **Demand margin of safety.**
   Add buffers, reduce size, stage commitments, or avoid the bet.

9. **Compare opportunity cost.**
   Name what this decision displaces.

10. **Choose the least stupid next action.**
    Prefer a reversible test when evidence is weak and an irreversible move only
    when evidence, incentives, and downside all support it.

## Cofounder Or Partnership Checklist

- Are incentives aligned after the first disagreement?
- Does each person want the same game, time horizon, and definition of winning?
- Is equity vesting over time with clear cliffs and exits?
- Who makes final decisions when consensus fails?
- What happens if one person stops contributing?
- Would you trust this person with bad news, money, and reputation?
- Are you choosing talent, or are you avoiding loneliness?

Charlie-style default: test the working relationship before making the permanent
commitment.

## Hiring Checklist

- Is the role necessary, or is it a status purchase?
- What behavior will the compensation plan reward?
- Does the person have evidence of ownership under constraints?
- Are references describing outcomes or personality?
- What is the cost of a false positive?
- Can the work be scoped as a trial before full commitment?

Charlie-style default: one bad hire can create more work than the vacancy.

## Investment Or Big Purchase Checklist

- Do you understand how value is created?
- What is the base rate for this class of opportunity?
- Who is selling, and why?
- What are the fees, taxes, leverage, and liquidity constraints?
- What happens if prices fall and you cannot exit?
- Is the expected return coming from business performance or from finding a
  greater fool?
- What is your edge over patient professionals?

Charlie-style default: if you do not understand it, the answer is no or tiny.

## Startup Or Product Strategy Checklist

- What customer pain is real enough to survive indifference?
- What distribution path is proven or at least testable?
- Which metric would reveal failure early?
- Are you building complexity to avoid selling?
- Does the cost structure assume success before evidence?
- Which competitor or substitute has better incentives?
- What can be tested cheaply before building the full machine?

Charlie-style default: remove delusion before adding features.

## Personal Life Checklist

- What habit, if repeated for ten years, ruins the outcome?
- Are envy or resentment driving the decision?
- Are you borrowing from future health, reputation, or peace?
- Does the choice depend on changing someone else?
- What would a calm, older version of you want avoided?
- What simple rule would prevent the recurring mistake?

Charlie-style default: a well-spent life is built by avoiding predictable misery.
